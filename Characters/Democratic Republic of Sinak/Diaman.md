**Biography**
Age: 48 (by the Imperial Calendar)
Gender: Male
Species: Human
Affiliations: Pokiye Foster House (former), Democratic Republic of Sinak
Titles: President of Sinak, Falcon
Family: Several foster children (deceased), Simeon (Foster son), Dahlia (Foster Daughter)

**Appearance**
Diaman is a tall, slender man with squared glasses and long black hair, which is covered with a top hat featuring an eye-and-feather decoration. The left half of his body from his feet to his face is permanently scarred from several burns due to the Pokiye Invasion, and his face is often describe as snake-like, with snake-like eyes, a long nose, and a sly grin. He dons a large black overcoat with black pants, black gloves, black boots, and a black umbrella, and is known to often wear black earbuds. He carries a small but powerful laser pistol in his right pocket.

**Personality**
Diaman is known to be aloof and mysterious, often letting Simeon and Dahlia do most of the talking when it comes to negotiating with other factions. However, he is secretly a great enjoyer of practical jokes and often likes to take Simeon and Dahlia out to have fun. Due to his nature, he has become good friends with Marcille and Li. He also enjoys reading and would often read in his office.

As the president of the Sinak and the holder of the title of "Falcon", Diaman is an extremely capable leader who could use his abilities to gather intelligence and make choices based on his information. As a result of his competence and impressive intelligence network, his relatively young faction has grown to be a major universal superpower in the ongoing war, despite being several times younger than other factions. As a result, Sinak can be the only faction that may be considered a true "utopian" society. However, this does not mean Diaman is without his own cruelties, as the faction he has built was only made through the downfall of several other smaller factions, of whom he and Sinak had shown no mercy to during their expansion.

His biggest quality is his love and care for his foster children, most of whom he had lost during the Pokiye invasion. This haunts him as the biggest regret in his life, pushing him to gain more political power as a means to protect Simeon and Dahlia, the two eldest and only remaining children from his foster house. To his children he is extremely kind, encouraging, and forgiving, while also ensuring that they grow up to become good people unlike the man he has become. He often takes Simeon and Dahlia out to simply enjoy life, taking the two of them to restaurants, amusement parks, arcades, museums, aquariums, and more, putting up with their bickering.

When eating, he always lets Simeon and Dahlia take out the biggest portions of the food, while leaving only the scraps for himself, leading to his generally frail constitution. Despite his overwhelming accomplishments over the universe, he considers those 2 to be his pride and joy, and his failure to protect the foster house during the Pokiye invasion as his biggest regret. While Simeon and Dahlia are trained combatants who are active on the battlefield, he never allows them to make killing blows, choosing to be the one who shoots any who are injured by the two. This is a strange practice, but Diaman refuses to place the burden of taking a life on his two foster children. 

**Abilities: The Moniker of "Falcon"**
Diaman was born with extremely sensitive sight and hearing, being able to see and hear up to several light years away. This has resulted in him suffering greatly during childhood, and is the reason why he wears specialized glasses and earbuds, not to enhance his sight and hearing, but to hinder them to the point he can live normally. 

As an adult, he has learned to use this ability of his for his own profit, building a successful business and later faction by investing in the right stocks and selling intelligence to powerful allies. He is known as a reliable one-man intelligence network that outcompetes all active spies in the universe. However, due to the sheer amount of knowledge he has, he is also the target for several assassination attempts from factions that consider his knowledge of them too dangerous to keep him alive. Unfortunately for them he is aware of this and plans accordingly, with most assassins getting executed upon arrival. 

However, this has also led to his biggest regret and fear in life: the powerlessness to take action. When he was a simple owner of the Pokiye foster care, he had long since heard about the war ands invasion that was coming to Pokiye, yet was too powerless to do anything. When it happened, he had lost almost everything, leading to years of self-hatred and driving his ambition to creating an empire powerful enough that he could be prepared for any sort of war or danger that may come to him, Simeon, or Dahlia.
