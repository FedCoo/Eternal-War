**Appearance**
Fabien is an extremely tall and muscular young-looking man with long white eyelashes, thick eyebrows, and what is described as a rough and "eternally angry" expression on his face. He has short, unkempt white hair and is always seen wearing a red Hawaiian shirt and shades. 

During his life on earth, he is portrayed as an old and feeble man in a lab coat, with the same short and unkempt white hair.

**Personality**
Fabien comes off as a brash and generally rude person who seems to always act or speak before he thinks. This has lead him to come off as nothing but a stupid brute to many who meet and greet him. However, he is also respected as being one of the few neutral sovereigns in the universe, and his innovations have made him infamous across the several factions. The reality that most people know is that Fabien is a genius unlike any other, making countless innovations that has shaped the structure of human society. As a result, many endure his often rude remarks, knowing the sheer influence he has on the universe as a whole. 

Fabien is also a lonely man, being the only person in the universe who is both immortal and invincible, a secret that many faction rulers vied for, and one that he has kept for himself for several long eons. The reason is not out of selfishness, but rather out of a sense of duty, for he alone has experienced the isolation that comes as a consequence of true immortality. However, despite being able to end his life at any point in time, he continues to live on as a way to keep on contributing to humanity and the universe as a whole. His ultimate goal is to somehow prevent the heat death of the universe, working endlessly on an unknown project to achieve that goal. He currently lives alone on FCS-789501, surrounded by nothing but artificial intelligence and machines in order to lift his lonely heart. 

Having been living in the universe for so long, Fabien is also extremely wise, understanding the pointlessness and futility of war, something he has experienced several times. From being a military researcher in Russia to the modern eon as a sovereign during the Eternal War, Fabien has gone through and suffered through trillions of different wars, yet he continues to create and contribute to humanity with the belief that there will come a day the Eternal War will end, and humanity can truly flourish with his innovations. He is also extremely caring to his children and his adopted child, Catherine, and is loyal to his wife during his time as a Russian scientist, refusing to fall in love again and to forever remember her in his heart. 

**Abilities: The Moniker of "Innovation"**
Fabien is a once-in-a-universe genius, having created several universe-changing innovations and being the only human to achieve immortality, invincibility, and the ability to access the 10th dimension, allowing him to see and manipulate the totality of all possibilities. This latter part however, comes at the cost of the plausibility of the existence of the universe, meaning the more he tries to access the 10th dimension, the less of the universe starts to exist. This leads to him rarely if ever accessing the 10th dimension, as he cannot bear the weight of taking a chunk of the universe out of the realm of possibility. The usefulness of his innovations has lead to him being one of the most respected people in the universe, as well as leading to him being the richest and in a way most powerful man in the universe, with several factions swearing to protect him and stand by him whenever he requires them to do so.

This does not mean he needs their help in any way, for his base alone is home to the most advanced military weaponry and security systems, making it impossible for any factions to even invade him. Most factions that do attempt to invade his planet simply become scattered particles of plausibility upon simply attempting to go through his barrier. Even if they are able to bypass this barrier, which is impossible without his permission, they would be bombarded with several bombs, each giving the same energy as a gamma ray burst. 

Through the use of the 4th dimension, Fabien has retained himself as a constant in time, meaning that he will never age no matter how many eons pass. He is also a well of infinite energy, allowing his body to move and regenerate no matter what kinds of fatal wounds he suffered. These are all secrets that he guards safely in order to ensure no one suffers like he does. 

At all times he has a mini robot that he carries on his person at all times, which can be used to summon his military technology and plausibility-scattering field anywhere he goes if he is in danger.
