**Abilities**
"Godslayer Spirit": The signature martial technique developed by Aiden, utilizing several different weapons and every possible function of the human body, most especially the amygdala, which is the human sense of fear and panic. Because of this, sovereigns will never be able to use this martial technique as a result of a lack of a proper amygdala. This is intentionally designed when Aiden developed it, due to his hatred for the Sovereigns and wish to never allow his skills to be ever tainted or learned by sovereigns. 

The Godslayer Spirit is, without a doubt, the single strongest martial technique in the universe, being able to outclass the extreme fighting strength, skills, and even impossibilities of sovereign due to its functions.
