**Biography**
Age: 24 (by the Imperial Calendar)
Gender: Male
Species: Human
Affiliations: Unknown Planet (Former), Unknown Brothel (former), HEIMDALL
Titles: HEIMDALL, Chief
Family: Unnamed parents (unknown)

**Appearance**
Lucien has messy blond hair which covers his blue eyes and is generally seen wearing glasses on top of his head. He does not need glasses. It is a fashion statement. He wears a black turtleneck with a grayish-blue overcoat, and black gloves, pants, and boots which covers his entire body. He has a lip ring, and several earrings which he has to take off during missions due to the noise they make. 

**Personality**
As the founder and Chief of the outlawed reporting and intelligence agency HEIMDALL, there is nothing Lucien values more than the truth. Having once been practically a slave to the elites of his home planet, he strongly believes that all members of the elite and corrupt, depraved, and overall inhumane, holding a strong hatred for any person of high privilege. He finds it unacceptable how uninformed the common man is of the world of the elite, and thus simply accepts their lives of oppression and poverty in the universe. 

As a result, he takes it upon himself to force transparency upon the universe, risking his life to infiltrate the world of the elites and the ugly politics of the factions. This ranges from noble households to military strongholds, in which he gathers information, pictures, and any other kinds of evidence he can gather before releasing them to the rest of the universe. He also actively goes around to instigate the people with the release of information, realizing that simply knowing of corruption is not enough to inspire people to act against it.

To his close friends, he is known for constantly wearing a frown, yet talking to him reveals that he is generally extremely jovial and lighthearted, often laughing and joking around. Even during his work, he is known to take things very seriously but still stick to a light and unprofessional manner, often resulting in comedic situations and banters.

It should be noted that Lucien is homosexual


**Abilities:**
Lucien is decent at a dual-wielding style of combat, utilizing a single knife and a pistol.

His true abilities lie in his ability to infiltrate different places, being a master of disguise, being able to change his appearance, voice, and even his entire demeanor at a whim. Beyond that he is known to be nigh undetectable when hiding.

He also employs several gadgets and is incredibly resourceful and aware of the environment whenever he is in combat or while infiltrating.

He is also shown to be incredibly intelligent and charismatic, successfully prompting many revolutions and always finding ways to spread information to the rest of the universe. 

# Story
**Early Life**
Lucien was born to an unnamed Duke of the planet Bughen, a planet which belonged to the previously known Empire of Sinak, living a generally privileged life surrounded by several servants who attended to his many needs. During his childhood, he was allowed to explore the vast estate of his father's house, yet was often unable to due to the tight schedule forced upon him by his father, who often made him study under several different tutors. While these tutors were sent to instruct him in arts, music, writing, and the sort, he was mostly interested in the stories of his cosmography tutor, a wizened old lady and a devout Terrazitonist who had thousands of tales to tell of her adventures in the universe in her search of Terra. From these stories Lucien became obsessed with the universe beyond the planet of Bughen, dreaming to become a Terrazitonist explorer just like his cosmography tutor.

When he turned 14, Lucien was enrolled into the prestigious Menschenmegalist Royal Academy of Udourgh. There, he was often treated well by his classmates, who were mostly lower members of nobility, due to his high social standing, yet he could not help but feel uneasy from the attention, realizing that these students are in a way, forced by their own lower social standings to be friends with Lucien, the son of a duke. 

One day, while Lucien was reading for an assignment at his academy, a ball struck him in the face. Looking up, he saw the terrified faces of his classmates playing baseball, all of whom were ready to prostrate before him besides one boy, who simply walked up to him and asked for the ball back. Surprised, Lucien asked the boy who his father was, believing him to be the prince of Sinak, only to find out he is a peasant boy who had gotten into the academy on a scholarship.

After this interaction, he begun to often look for Sytra while in the academy, wishing to befriend him. While Sytra never searched for Lucien's company, he never minded Lucien either, and after a few months, the two of them had become friends. Lucien was enamored with Sytra, whom despite his peasant upbringing, was a boy who truly seemed "free" in the eyes of Lucien. He treats the other students with nothing but simple decency, and was often beaten up for never showing respect to his peers who were of a higher social class than him. He often skipped class whenever he didn't feel like it only to go to the next class simply because he liked the teacher, played simple pranks whenever he wanted, and never studied, yet often scored at the top of the class, living his life however he wanted, ignoring the rules of Sinakii society. Soon, Lucien realized that he wished to be more than just friends to Sytra, yet he was unable to understand what was the feeling of longing he had whenever he talked to the peasant boy.

At some point in their second year, Lucien realized he had fallen in love with Sytra, and asked Sytra out. While Sytra did not harbor similar feelings at the time, he was willing to date Lucien, thinking that he may fall in love during the relationship. By the end of the second year, Sytra had also fallen in love with Lucien.

In their 3rd year, a biochemically engineered famine from the Ingvis Empire spread over the planet of Bughen. The nobility raised the taxes on the working people of Bughen, under the pretense that they have to sustain their military force in order to keep the people of Bughen safe. Due to these taxes, Sytra's father and his only family died to starvation while Sytra was still at the academy.

Sytra never received the news, only returning home at the end of the academy semester to find an eviction notice on the front door. Stepping in, all he found was his father's corpse, still decaying on the floor.

Afterwards, Sytra formed a club with Lucien at the Royal Academy named HEIMDALL, gathering several students of the lowest nobility who had also suffered in some way from the famine and the taxes. Through the club, Sytra attempted to make reforms within the academy, calling for fairer policies for the lower class students at the academy, with goals to expand beyond the academy and demand fairer policies from the nobles through political pressure. However, the club never gained real traction due to the comfortable life of the generally high-class students, who often shunned members of the club as "traitors" to the noble class. 

In order to put some actual pressure on the academy and nobles, Sytra began to work with Yuba, a 1st year Royal Academy student and member of HEIMDALL who was a prolific hacker and informations collector, to begin searching for information about the academy. Soon they were able to discovery a case of bribery between a teacher and student, and used it as leverage to call for small reforms within the classroom. Afterwards, Sytra and Lucien continued to search for evidence of corruption and other crimes committed within the academy, then within the nobility of Bughen, which they could spread to the working people, causing several nobles to lose their status and be overthrown. 

Eventually, in their 4th year, HEIMDALL had become more than a club, becoming an organization dedicated to releasing information of corruption among the noble class and mobilizing the workers of Sinak to overthrow the nobles that rule them. While at this point, the Sinak Empire's Safety Bureau had begun to try to search for the members of HEIMDALL, Sytra had Yuba wipe off all records of HEIMDALL from the Royal Academy, while ensuring that all members are protected on the SolarNet. 

However, Sytra's identity as the leader of HEIMDALL soon was leaked by a member of HEIMDALL, someone from a lower noble class who personally knew Sytra, and ratted out Sytra in order to gain a high social standing.

During their last interaction in the middle of Bughen's night, Sytra handed a Lucien his journal, kissing Lucien before giving a tearful farewell. The journal detailed Sytra's life starting from when he came to the Royal Academy, from his experiences at the school to his family, often referring to his life as a peasant boy living in poverty and seeing all the working members around him living under similar conditions. He wished to one day liberate the entire empire of Sinak from the idea of social class and from the rule of the nobility, wanting to only see the families around him happy and fulfilled, being able to truly earn what they worked for. The journal also detailed his time with Lucien, how he had felt indifferent to Lucien but did enjoy his company, his shock at Lucien asking him out, and how he eventually had fallen in love.

The very next day, Lucien saw Sytra's corpse crucified in front of the gates of the Royal Academy. Several soldiers stood around the body as the other students gathered around to throw food and other items at the corpse. Seeing all the food stuck to Sytra's corpse, all Lucien could think of was the ongoing famine. Going up close to Sytra's body, Lucien saw Sytra's face. 

The 17 year old boy had been executed by the state, alone and afraid.

It was a face Lucien would never forget, even years later. He would often dream of Sytra's head floating in front of him, forever locked into the terrified expression of a boy who once had dreams of freedom.

Later that night, Lucien withdrew trillions of credits from the Dukedom he was supposed to inherit. With a few close members of HEIMDALL, he bribed a mercenary ship and fled the planet and the Sinak Empire as a whole. Soon, Lucien began to continue the operations of HEIMDALL, with goals to liberate the people of several factions in the universe by helping them overthrow their respective authorities, dreaming of one day freeing the cosmos, just like how Sytra had tried to free Sinak.
