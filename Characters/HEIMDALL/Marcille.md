**Appearance**
Marcille is a tall, athletic woman who has short-shoulder length and messy hair with golden but discolored eyes. She dresses in gothic fashion, with a frilly poet shirt with and a sapphire brooch. She dons a general's coat over her shoulders and wears black gloves that do not cover her thumb, index, and middle fingers. She has high-waisted pants that go down to her ankles and Chelsea boots. She is constantly accompanied by a metal suitcase, which she has been shown to be extremely protective of, taking it with her everywhere and sometimes even treating it like her own child. She may sometimes have noticeable eyebags due to excessive crying. 

**Personality**

However, due to her time as a slave of the Ingvis Empire, she has a deep-seated hatred specifically for the Ingvis Empire, as well as the Rok'r tribe, whom she was born a part of before being sold to Ingvis by her family. The trauma from her experience there has made her unable to truly form any romantic connections with anyone, and besides with Li, she is generally distrustful of any and all men due to her background.

Despite holding the moniker of "Death", she places great value on human life, often trying to solve most problems with words before getting violent. She has a strong belief that all deaths should have a mourner, and is known to start crying before she fights, as to mourn those who will lose their lives to her.

**Abilities:**

Overview: As the Sovereign of Death, Marcille is undoubtedly the strongest fighter and likely the most powerful being in the Universe. All the while, she makes sure to keep the suitcase close to her at all times, not just due to an emotional attachment, but due to its ability to produce weapons various weapons utilized by her due to a strange ability from within the suitcase. Her set of sovereign abilities are considered by many to be the most powerful within existence, with each of them having destructive force accompanied by extreme precision and skill. 

*Plausibilities*
Extreme Strength: Marcille is well regarded for her extreme strength, being able to easily power just about any opponent, and even destroy entire planets with a single punch. 


*Possibilities*
"Warp": By preparing to flick her fingers, her sheer strength can generate enough potential energy that upon flicking, the energy released from her flick will collapse upon itself, creating a black hole which lasts for an instant (quite literally at absolutely zero seconds). By adjusting the force she uses between her fingers, she can adjust the size and power of the black hole, warping the reality before her in a single instant. This ability of hers has been used to instantly end people and planets before her on the spot, and due to its extremely short duration of a single instant, anything else that is not intended as a target will be unaffected by the instantaneous warp in reality.

"Scatter": Upon generating a potential black hole between her fingers, letting it collapse upon itself in and instant, and extinguishing it in that same exact instant of zero time, the energy intended to collapse upon the victim will instead enter a state of singularity reversal, causing the victim to enter into an impossible state of time, which simply causes them to scatter across reality. Similar to "Warp", it only appears for an instant and its size and power can be adjusted, in which by adjusting its power Marcille is able to adjust how far she lowers a person's probability of existence.

"Distort": By manipulating only the 3rd dimensional space through her ability to generate energy, she can distort the space around her to create new environmental variables, as well as distort people around her without exactly affecting them in any way. Essentially, the universe is made up of an infinite number of points, and Marcille is able to rearrange where each point is on the universe in order to shape the environment in ways that already existed to the universe in the first place, with a way to completely reset things to the way they originally were before her interferences.

"Extract": Marcille is able to extract out any tangible object that she can think of in her mind, allowing it to manifest in the form of pure energy. This extracted object cannot be consumed in any way, and would completely evaporate practically anyone and anything that touches it besides Marcille. It is potent to the point where she can build a whole city of energy that will vaporize anything on touch, but for the most part, she uses this ability to generate weapons and shoot on the spot. Since energy is something that exists in the universe, it is considered a possibility. 

*Impossibilities*
"Form 3 Impossibility: Erase": Marcille generates a point in space on her target which is considered impossible even beyond the realm of impossibility. This point of absolute impossibility leads to the collapse of all 10 dimensions upon the intended target, causing them to simply be completely erased from existence itself. This is the only known possible method of absolute erasure in existence that doesn't cause any consequences to the overall plausibility of the universe, and not even Marcille has been aware of her target's existence after using this attack.
