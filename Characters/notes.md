Characters have their own special abilities, whereas Sovereigns have an exceptionally powerful abilities. Exceptionally powerful sovereigns have an "impossibility", gained from the self-actualization of an individual who has a sovereign gene, thus realizing their true potential understanding what they truly wish to do with their life and abilities (this can be both good and evil). This impossibility is a kind of special ability that can cause effects that should not be possible in the universe. These impossibilities are mostly abstract.
