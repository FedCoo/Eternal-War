The Haud Empire is one of the most powerful empires in the entire universe, rivaling and even surpassing the Ingvis in terms of economy, land, military, and technology. Formed from a coalition of people who broke away and escaped from the clutches of the Ingvis, generations of genius and ruthless decisions allowed them to develop to the level of Ingvis. They are a major colonial force that continues to expand while warring against the Ingvis, using their moral superiority over the Ingvis as a main selling point to attract intellectuals and sovereigns contract under their operations. However, underneath their "moral war" against the Ingvis, they also continue to aggressively expand their empire, colonizing several planets and galaxies while directing all resources to the capitals and major trading hubs of the empire. 


## Infrastructure
The Haud empire's major planets and trading hubs often feature impressive eco-utopian architecture, showing off their wealth and greenery as a symbol of their pride and identity as the morally superior superpower of the universe. These efforts have been successful, as many other alliances within the universe praise them and establish trade with them with the idea of taking down the Ingvis empire.

## Politics
The Haud Empire is ruled mostly by the Haud Dynasty, with the emperor being the absolute ruler of all the galaxies and planets that are under the empire's control. Despite scientific advancements everywhere else, the Haud Empire chose to keep a more ancient system of kings and nobility, with several dukes ruling over galaxies while counts rule over clusters, whereas lower nobilities have rule over individual planets. Wealthy merchants and other smaller forms of aristocrats often also have their own large plots of land within each planet that they have control over. All members of the aristocracy answer to their superiors, and all must absolutely follow the orders of the king.  
There are courts and parliaments which enforce and pass laws, and are mostly used to maintain the image of a fair democracy. Yet any attempts to limit the power of the king often gets vetoed by the king, and is often not overridden due to the number of loyalists within Parliament.

Most of the economy of Haud goes into infrastructure, technology, and military, with a small amount going to foods and public services only for the the major trading hubs and sites. Most plantations and mining centers are heavily taxed for their resources and often are given only enough for its workers to survive the day. 

While there is a voting system in place for members of the parliament only, the crown is passed through lineage, and an individual's wealth will determine the power of their vote, with the votes of the rich having far more weight than any common person. 

## Military
The Haudian Empire's military is often noted to be one of the most powerful militaries in the cosmos, often surpassing the Ingvis military (#1) in certain statistics such as military population and benefits, alongside their control over supply lines throughout the universe. Each member of the Haudian military is also given a Haudian badge, which enhances their body to no longer require food or rest as long as the user has it on their body.

This badge comes at the cost of the user's lifespan, with each passing "death" they should have experience from a lack of basic nutrition being taken off the last of their lifespan. As a result, many Haudian veterans often die before receiving any of the promised benefits that should have been given to them by the Haudian government. Since the contract states that these benefits can only apply to veterans and their families who received them through application, many of these families often do not receive any benefits for their members joining the military. The avoid having to spend their budget on these generous military benefits, the Haudian computers often calculate a person's expected death date as long as they wear the Haudian badge, and discharge them on the day they are to die.
