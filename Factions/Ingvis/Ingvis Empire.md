**Overview**
The Ingvis Empire is one of the most powerful yet most despised empires in the universe. While it is true that they have a impressive military and technology, and they compete with the Haud Empire as a trade superpower, it comes at the cost of the people. The Ingvis empire is ran by the emperor Lotik and several nobles, who are known to make lavish spendings at the cost of the people. Yet Lotik is known to be shrewd, and has been able to decieve everyone by allowing them to believe that Ingvis is losing the war, despite them being a superpower. This causes the people to believe that their spendings all go to the military, which is only half true
