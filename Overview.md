**Setting:**
Far, far into the future, humanity has explored and populated most of the universe. Through the use of highly advanced technology, they have achieved faster-than-light travel, enhanced population genetics, longer lifespans, and much more. Humanity has also discovered several alien species in the universe and established trade and communications with them. However, there is an ongoing conflict that spans between the different factions of the universe, a war that is only known as "The Eternal War". It is unknown when exactly this war started due to the several different calendars these factions use, and it is unknown how this war started due to centuries of propaganda used by these different factions to bolster support for themselves in the ongoing war. Within these factions are a few very special people known across the universe as practically "gods" (they will be titled as "Sovereigns"), each of them with a Moniker that highlights their specialties. These people can either be loyal to a specific faction, or may be neutral, but they are simply special human beings and like the other characters, can make human emotions and connections. Due to their power and the violent politics of the universe, the non-neutral people are often chosen as representatives of their own factions when negotiating alliances, treaties, surrenders, and other forms of diplomacy.
