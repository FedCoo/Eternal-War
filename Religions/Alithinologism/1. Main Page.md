**Overview**

Alithinologism is a polytheistic religion that sees the ever increasing use of technology and development as humanity's holy guidance towards evolution. It is one of the largest and most popular religions in the universe, with almost 12% of the universe's population identifying as Alithinologists, making it have about over 52 trillion followers. Alithinologism is seperated into several smaller schools of Alithonologist thoughts, each with similar and different beliefs and practices

**Beliefs**
Members of Alithinologism have the fundamental belief that technology is the ultimate driving force towards human evolution
