While it is possible to have multiple classifications of species, we will divide them into 3 classes: Sentients, Animals, and Plants

Humans are the most advanced and populous species in the universe.

Sentients are defined as any species with conscious thought, with the most prominent being humans. However, since humanity has made up a majority of the universe, few factions have implemented laws defending the rights of non-human sentients. There are a few factions that have done this, namely the Democratic Republic of Sinak, and some activists that are working to implement these laws in their own factions.

Animals are defined as heterotrophic creatures. This means that even if it may look like a plant and have similar body structures to plants (e.g. a cell wall), it may still be classified as an animal if it cannot perform photosynthesis and relies on feeding off other living beings for energy.

Plants are defined as autotrophic creatures. This means that even if it may look and move like an animal and have similar body structures to animals (e.g. no cell wall), it may still be classified as a plant if it relies on photosynthesis or other form of self-production for energy. 

All Sovereigns are sentient, but not all are human. Most however, are human.
