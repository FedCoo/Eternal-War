A sovereign is a human with a specific mutation that gives them superhuman abilities and enhanced physical attributes. This can include super-strength, super-speed, extremely combat capabilities, enhanced intelligence, and much more. While most sovereigns have different mutations that gave them these abilities, they have all widely been classified under the wing of a "Sovereign Mutation". Due to the extremely large size of the human population within the universe, having populated most of the known universe, there are billions if not trillions of people who may have the sovereign mutation within the universe.

Mutations classified as a sovereign mutation is something that is caused by a rare glitch of reality during the process of creating a human within a human's DNA sequence before birth, a mutation so rare that it has only begun to appear around 200 million years ago. These mutations are known for destroying limiters on the human body and human existence, causing a person to be born with special abilities that defy both human and existential laws. These abilities can then be honed by the bearer of the sovereign mutation. Some mutations are superior over others, making the physiology of sovereigns inherently unequal. 

These mutations cannot be inherited through reproduction between a sovereign mutation holder and another being.

In some cases, people who hold mutations do not awaken the mutation until a certain point in time in their lives, as the mutation may not have fully grown in their bodies yet.

While many sovereigns live regular lives, many sovereigns also make their claim to fame with their powers. These sovereigns are granted concepts which people believe they embody, such as the "Sovereign of Death", Marcille, whom people who have heard of her have associated her and her feats with the concept of death. Some ordinary humans who commit incredible feats may be designated a title of a sovereign and a concept as well.

A sovereign's abilities are divided into three different sections: Plausibilities, Possibilities, and Impossibilities.

Plausibilities are feats of strength and skill that while certainly impressive to a majority of the universe, could be achieved by extremely advanced human genetics, enhancements, and millennia of training in anti-temporal chambers.
Possibilities are abilities that are considered impossible by any sort of human standard, even with genetics and technology, but do not break the laws of the universe and dimensional causality.
Impossibilities are abilities that shatter the laws of universe and dimensional causality. Despite breaking the flow of causality within the dimensional realms, they do not eat away at the overall causality of existence, unlike other non-sovereign reality-warping technologies, hence why they are considered impossible.

Sovereigns generally have 3 impossibilities in their skillset:
Form 1 Impossibilities are generally the weakest ones, with existential alterations which could easily be reversed and brought back to normal by the Sovereign
Form 2 Impossibilities are powerful enough to make small and irreversible alterations to the universe and existence as a whole, but not large enough to have a huge impact on anything
Form 3 Impossibilities are powerful enough to break dimensional laws and cause irreversible changes to existential flows without affecting any laws of causality that may destroy reality as a whole. 

Currently, many scientists are trying to recreate the mutation to artificially engineer sovereigns, all attempts have ended in failure due to the existential nature of these mutations, which prevents artificial sovereigns from being able to gain a set of impossibilities. The only known successful artificial sovereign was Fabien, the sovereign of Innovation, who has taken great care to ensure that any and all of his methods are held to the utmost secrecy.
