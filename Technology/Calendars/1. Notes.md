The universe is currently in the Technologeic Eon, Cenoanthroic Era, Secondary Period, and Guerrainios Epoch.

For the sake of simplicity we will assume that the universe uses the system of years, months, and days in terms of calculating rotations and revolutions.

Even though planets have different rotations and revolutions, the calendars used differ by faction rather than location. Instead, planets of a particular faction may use a separate equation on the standard calendar of their faction to offset this discrepancy. This can be considered a sort of "Calendar Zone", similar to how each planets have their own time zones. 
	For Example:
	**Planet X belongs to a faction that uses a 16-month, 40-day standard calendar**. However:
	Planet X’s **local day** is half the standard day for this faction's system.
    Planet X’s **revolution** around its star is only **10 standard months** in this faction's system.
    Planet X must maintain the faction's standard calendar
    To do this, Planet X will first determine how many days it has in a year.
    Since a day in the standard calendar is 2 days on Planet X, then rather than having 640 days in a year, Planet X would have 1280 days in a year.
    However, since Planet X only takes 10 months in this way, then we would multiply the number of Planet X days by 0.625 (or 10/16)
    This means Planet X has 800 days
    Since we have 16 months on the calendar, then planet X would have 50 planet X days in each month rather than the 40 standard days.
    This means when calculating days from the standard calendar to planet X, we would use a multiplier of 1.25
    For example, if we are looking at the 4th month and 20th day on the standard calendar, we would be looking at the 4th month and 25th day on Planet X.
    Essentially: **Planet X Day = Standard Day x 1.25**
Due to the sheer level of calculations needed to accommodate for each planet of each faction's standard calendar used, we will pretend that the mathematicians of each planet and faction have already taken these discrepancies into account, allowing allied planets and factions to trade and communicate with each other clearly. 

For the sake of simplicity we will assume the entire universe uses the system of seconds, minutes, and hours.

Time zones for each planet are calculated based on the planet's equator being a standard time and adding or subtracting a 1 hour offset depending on how far we are from the planet's equator.
