This calendar originates from a planet whose days go by in 16 hours, and years go by in 160.85 days. Rather than using the Imperial Calendar, mathematicians have devised the Octosolaric Calendar for this planet, using an 8-month 20-day system.

A leap year is every 20 years in this calendar, where every month gets 2 extra days while the 8th month gets 3

Several factions whose base planet follows an almost similar system of rotations and revolutions have adopted this calendar system, ignoring any slight or even noticeable differences in their own planet's cycle compared to the origin planet.
