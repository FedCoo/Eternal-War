**Developer**
Fabien

**Used by**
The universe

**Summary**
Due to the size of the universe, it is now inefficient and impossible to use only signals to send and receive data. In order to make communication efficient again, devices now use faster-than-light Tachyon particles[^1]

**Notes**
- IP Addresses now use 256-bit integers to account for the number of devices (people used, AI, systems, everything device-related) in the universe
- Most of everything is handled by the FCS-400982546281, or what is widely known as the SolarNet
	- This is a universe-sized artificial intelligence supercomputer that has been sent to the 4th dimension in order to prevent it from interfering and causing catastrophic damages in the 3rd-dimensional space. Essentially, its existence in the 4th dimension is a single constantly moving point in time that will always be in the future, relative to our time, thus forever existing outside of our time. It's sole purpose is to receive and transfer data from Device A to Device B. It can detect all created devices in the universe and route it to itself, before sending it to the intended receiver. In order to send Tachyon particles to this 4th dimension, there are portals to the 4th dimension install in each manufactured device. These portals are stabilized 0-dimensional "points" which allow tachyons to enter the 4th dimension without interfering with 3-dimensional matter

**Workings**
1. Device A prepares data that is to be sent to Device B.
2. Device A breaks down the data into chunks, which are then encrypted into multiple Tachyon particles
3. These Tachyon particles are sent to their respective spot in the 4th-dimensional universe, and are then transmitted to the SolarNet computer, which checks that all Tachyon data particles are present before sending it to the respective location of the point portal for Device B in the 4th dimension. 
4. Device B in the 3rd dimension then receives these particles and after ensuring that all particles are present, decrypts and reconstructs the data to be received by Device B
5. All of this happens in a millisecond.

[^1]: Tachyon particles are a real thing in theoretical physics, check it out!
