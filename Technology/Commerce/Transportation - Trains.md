**Developer**
Catherine

**Used By**
Most of the Universe

**Summary**
An evolved form of the current bullet trains, most planets use a system of hover-lights, large pill-shaped vehicles that move at the speed of light to transport people between different locations on a planet, and even between planets on an empire.

**Notes**
- Each hover-light is shaped like a smooth, long, and gray pill-shaped vehicle with windows on the side
- The pill is an encasing for millions of particles of light being in an unobserved state, essentially a form of "captured light"
- The pill is moved by the light within the capsule, which is moved by having an outside force cast a shadow on the pill. This shadow is transferred into an equation through the pill's system and send to the light particles as a simulated shadow that does not directly interfere with the light in an unobserved state. This causing the light to shoot out away from the simulated shadow until the simulation stops when the destination is reached,

**Workings**
1. People get inside the pill (first, business, and economic class) and take a seat
2. Conductor directs a shadow through a computer, and enters the location it wishes to go to.
3. The shadow is converted into an equation, used into a simulation that does not interfere with the light particles while still causing them to act
4. The light particles within the pill shoots out, causing the pill to move at the speed of light
5. Once the pill reaches its destination, the simulation detects this and ends it.
