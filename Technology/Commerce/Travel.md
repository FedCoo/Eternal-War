**Summary**
Due to the size of the universe, it is now inefficient and impossible to travel to places as everything is light years away. To achieve faster than-light-travel, we will assume and apply the following:
- The concept of the observer effect
- That humanity, due to its long history in the universe, has graphed the entire universe in a 3-dimension coordinate plane

**Notes**
- Each spaceship is large mostly due to the size of the supercomputer within it that calculates coordinates in the universe. Larger spaceships means that it can host more crewmates. It can also make longer jumps as it can compute larger graphs of the universe due to a larger supercomputer size. 
	- Smaller ships can make regional jumps across planets or solar systems
	- Larger ships can make jumps across different galaxies
	- The largest ships can make jumps across galaxy clusters, and the single largest ship can move to opposite ends of the observable universe
- Even small ships can travel long distances, they simply have to make more jumps and have a greater risk of scattering into infinite possibilities as a result.
- The 3rd dimensional coordinate graph of the universe does account for the forever expanding universe, and generates graphs accordingly. It is similar to how we can almost infinitely expand the desmos graph
- Spaceships are mass-produced in the modern day, but the concept behind the innovation is known as the FCS-8900443674
- We will assume that interactions can only happen in the 3D space, so in the 4D space humans do not count as observers but rather as part of the spaceship
- Pilots enter the location using a large holographic map of the universe that can be zoomable. Specific planets can also be searched and entered. They simply have to point to the space they wish to go to and the computer will calculate its coordinates. They are trained to know planets and be able to handle these complex maps.
- There exists within the ship a causal shielding field, the purpose of this is to keep the crewmates safe as they are in the 4D space
- If there is something that goes wrong, it will be likely that the crewmates will simply scatter into infinite possibilities. There have been recorded instances of this happening and is caused by internal damage to the ships.

**Workings**
1. Driver enters the destination, the supercomputer calculates its coordinates
2. Ship phases into the 4th dimensional space
3. In this 4th dimensional space, the ship and its crewmates are considered as "unobserved" and is currently in a state of infinite possibilities, being anywhere and everywhere at once. 
4. The supercomputer in the ship uses the coordinates to pinpoint the exact possibility it wishes to be at, and collapses itself into that specific possibility, phasing back into the 3D space in the specified coordinates
5. This is done instantaneously
