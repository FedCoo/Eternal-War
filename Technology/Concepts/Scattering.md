**Overview**
"Scattering into probability" is a concept utilized to negate the impact of an individual's existence on the entropy of the universe. Since erasing an individual from existence is impossible without accessing the 10th Dimension or disrupting the causality of the universe, scattering is a method where a person's "probability of existence" is simply lowered to the point where they may not even be considered to have existed. This is done through manipulating their states in the 4th Dimensional plane.

**Explanation**
Consider a person's existence to be a correlational graph with time on the x-axis and state (a person's state of existence e.g. action and location) in the y-axis. Their birth is their 0th state at the 0th time, and the slope of the graph is "impact" (defined as the level of change they make to the entropic levels of the universe). When their existence is plotted on the scatterplot, we will get a scatterplot in the positive, linear direction with an exact r-value of 1.

With the equation *y = a + bx*, we can measure a person's existence as:
#### *state = birth + (impact x time)*
With the r-value relating to a person's "probability"
This graph essentially demonstrates that when a person is in a past state, it occurred in a past time; when a person is in a present state, it occurred in a present time; and when a person is in a future state, it occurred in a future time. For every one unit of time that passes by, their state of existence increases by one unit of impact. In a normal person's life, this fact remains unchanging.

When a person scatters into probability, however, we lower their r-value or "probability"  of existence to become closer to 0, resulting in a graph more like this:
![[Chart (1).png]]This graph demonstrates that a person's past, present, or future state may occur at a past, present, or future time. A person in their past state may occur during a present time, person in their future state may occur during a past time, and much more. This scattering of their states over time lowers their "probability" of existence. For every one unit of time that passes by, their state of existence may jump.
For example, a soldier who has scattered may suddenly be in a state of their childhood inside a family home, whereas in the future where they are supposed to grow old and tending to animals in their farm, they are in a state of being at war on a different plant, and in the past when they are in their childhood inside a family home, they are an old man tending to animals in their farm. 

**Possible (tragic) scenario/example**
Somebody's lover is scattered into probability; they seemed to have simply disappeared in the blink of an eye, as if they never existed except in that somebody's mind; they come to the conclusion that their lover has not vanished out of existence and decide to search. They search for many years throughout the entire universe to no avail.

One day, they decide to go to the place where they first fell in love to reminisce. When they arrive at the location, they see their lover, in the exact same location with the exact same appearance doing exactly what they were doing many years ago, when they first fell in love. Suddenly, the lover vanishes.

The person searching did not see a vision of their lover, but rather in the present moment, witnessed their lover in a past state of existence.
