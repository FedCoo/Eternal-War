**Developer**
Catherine

**Used by**
Several large factions, even those that are enemies the Haud, the faction it was developed in. Also used by normal citizens for exploration and other quality-of-life purposes

**Summary**
The QSD or Quantum Storage Device allows users to scan and log items into the device, which perfectly copies the data of the scanned item and puts it in its storage. When a user wishes to use a logged item, it uses energy to create a working replica of the item, which can be used until the device runs out of batteries

**Notes**
- The device uses a quantum scanner to record the atomic structure and functional state of the scanned object
- The device converts energy to matter to create items and allow them to be functional. Once it runs out of energy, the item created simply disperses into particles
- There can be no single item that can be logged by multiple devices. As a result, producing the actual item is still necessary for each device. This is due to the fact that devices would place an imprint on an item scanned, which interferes with other devices scanning the exact same item. This imprint is created through the communications technology and links the logged item to the device, it can be removed if the item is deleted from the device logs
- The device cannot handle living beings due to their chaotic quantum states
- The device requires both batteries and a liquid coolant to have usable energy and to cool it the device due to the heat generated by energy
- The logged object that is replicated is not real and is held by energy and a stability field. Once the device runs out of batteries it will simply dissipate. 
- If the device is not sufficiently cooled, it can explode, destroying the device
- Device size does matter. Larger devices have larger storages for data and thus can log more items to replicate. Smaller, more portable devices have smaller storage and can log less items. Depending on the size, there are also larger explosions if it overheats
- There exists pre-logged versions of the device on the market, such as for a space exploration survivalist or first aid kit. These are non-customizable, meaning the logged items cannot be deleted, and each item can only be used a certain number of times (a number that is also logged to each item in the device UI). However, they are cheaper than customizable devices.

**Workings**
1. A user uses the device to scan an object. The device leaves an imprint to connect the object to itself, and stores the quantum data of the device in its logs
2. When the uses wishes to use the scanned object, the device uses energy to replicate the item. It also uses liquid coolant to cool itself down
3. Once the device runs out of batteries or is turned off, the replica is simply dissipated into heat
