A majority of the technology in here is developed by Fabien, the sovereign of Innovation, and Catherine, Fabien's only student and the head of the Haud Empire's Research and Development Department.

The fact Fabien has a student is known only to him and Catherine, the said student.

Technologies developed by Fabien have been standardized to the entire universe, regardless of faction differences, due to Fabien's longstanding neutral reputation as well as the fact that his inventions are all extremely reliable, with most major factions relying on his technologies. 

Technologies developed by Fabien usually have their own names. However, their official name from Fabien is FCS-N, with N denoting that this technology is Fabien's Nth invention

There is universe-wide speculation on what FCS stands for. It stands for "Fabien's Cool Stuff" (according to Fabien). However, most people do not know this.

Technologies developed by Catherine is used by the Haud Empire, allied factions, and enemy factions that have either stolen the designs or buying it while unknowingly boosting the economy of the Haud Empire. 

Other technologies are developed by inventors of other factions, and are often less standardized, to either keep a monopoly on an innovation or simply due to a lack of communication and trade with other factions. 

Several technologies mention the use of a 4th dimension. We will establish this 4th dimension as time, and shifting into the 4th dimension means manipulating its existence in time.
