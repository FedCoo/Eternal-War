Due to the size of the universe, we will only be noting important planets, regions, and buildings

Planets will be defined as large, naturally-created celestial bodies that orbit around a certain star. Most planets are habitable now due to advanced human genetics

Regions are defined as specific locations (cities or areas in general) that may be notable either due to their large economic power (e.g. Wall Street on earth), significant natural resources, or landmark events (such as a notable battle breaking out in a specific region).

Buildings are defined as man-made structures that serve a specific purpose, whether it be practical or symbolic. These can be as small as notable houses or castles and as large as man-made megastructures that can even be the size of a planet.
