**Overview

Novico is a hyper-capitalist techno-feudalist region defined by its tourist locations and innovative technology. Novico planet belongs to the Novi Conglomerate, a major supplier and financier of the Ingvis Empire. The region is made up of 5% wealthy tourists who often cycle in and out of the planet, 10% white collar workers and executives of the Novi Conglomerate. 90% of the population lives underground, acting as underpaid and overworked members of the working class of Novico. 

Novico is controlled by the Novi Conglomerate, and its executives, who manage different sectors and branches of the area. Each sector specializes in a certain part of Novi Conglomerate's industries, ranging from foods to entertainment to military technologies, with each sector having tourist attractions themed around the specific industries. These executives have absolute power over these regions, with small amount of autonomy given to the white collars working directly under the executives. 

While Novico does not go under any conflicts and is generally safe from most wars, it suffers from a major humanitarian crisis, as most of its citizens are members of the workforce who has never seen the light of day ever in their lives.

**Geography**
The tourist center and major production planet of the Novi Corp, Novico, belongs to the Hauyt Galaxy of the Iordian Cluster, also known as the galaxies of the Ingvis Empire. While the actual planet of Novico is barren and mostly polluted due to the thousands of factories. However, the inner ring of Novico has an artificial atmosphere and environments that makes the tourist center seem much more appealing that it actually is. The planet itself is huge, being about 10x the diameter of the old Terra (earth).

Novico is divided into the outer ring and the inner ring, with the Marble Tower in the center. The Outer Ring is on the surface of the planet, which hosts the workers and laborers of Novico, who are ultimately unable to ever leave the planet. On the outer ring is the entrance to the inner ring, where ships enter and leave the inner ring to dock. The outer ring is a land filled with factories and a heavily polluted atmosphere. There is practically no nature within the Outer Rings and every part of it is filled with factories and living spaces for the residents/laborers of the Outer Rings. Any trace of nature the once lush planet had has been fully industrialized by the Novi Corp.

Entering from the crater of the Outer Rings, tourists and businessmen will enter the world of the Inner Ring. It is a giant hollow spherical area with buildings all around the planet. Looking in any direction, even upwards, one will simply see buildings lying sideways and upside down, held together by the artificial gravity of the Inner Ring. The air itself is also much fresher and nicer within the Inner Ring.

**Infrastructure**
The Outer Ring of Novico is filled with pollution coming from gigantic factories around the planet. People of the Outer Rings live in apartments that surround each factory within a 10-block radius. 

At the center of the hollow planet is the Marble Tower, a giant pillar that crosses across the x-y-z centers of the hollow planet. This acts as not just a set of elevators to transport people and vehicles. Within the Marble Tower is a luxury set of massive penthouses, residential areas for the chiefs and executives of Novi Corp. Each penthouse is about as large as an entire castle, and there are several luxury facilities and recreation centers that are enjoyed by the residents of these penthouses. Navigating the Marble Tower is incredibly simple, with each executive having a special vehicle for them, which has a self driving mode they can turn on or off depending on whether or not they plan to drive around the facility. This tower is considered the ultimate goal of the businessmen and white collar workers of Novico, and residence inside it is a symbol of respect and authority within the planet.
